# Product review templates

Use only sections useful to the decision. Unknown values remain explicit.

## Capability baseline
| Capability / exact scope | Implemented evidence | Technical verification | Deployment / live acceptance | Adoption / outcome evidence | Remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Record source links, revision/date, confidence and conflicting records. Do not collapse the columns into one "done" flag.

## Opportunity assessment
- Customer/buyer and problem:
- Evidence, source/date and confidence:
- Intended customer/business outcome:
- Options: smallest build, discovery, existing-flow improvement, workaround, defer:
- Impact, strategic fit and urgency:
- Effort range, estimator and confidence:
- Dependencies, support cost and operational risk:
- Recommendation, displaced work and reconsideration trigger:

## Roadmap
| Horizon | Customer outcome | Evidence / confidence | Smallest slice | Dependencies / capacity assumption | Success measure / decision gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Now = selected current focus; Next = conditional priority; Later = options.
Do not infer committed dates or owner approval from horizon placement.
